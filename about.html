About Enga Tour Guide

A local business sharing the places and culture of Enga Province.

Who we are

Enga Tour Guide was started to help visitors enjoy Enga Province with people who know it best. Our guides, scouts and drivers are from local communities. We work with landowners and villagers so that tourism benefits the people who look after these sites.

Our mission

To offer safe, well organised and respectful tours to the Enga Salt Pond, the Enga Cultural Show, mountain tracks and bird watching locations, while keeping the benefits of tourism in local hands.

Who we serve

Our customers are both national and international visitors of every age. Which tour site they choose depends on what they want to see and do.

Our values

  • Respect for local customs, land and sacred places
  • Safety on every track and road
  • Fair pay for scouts, drivers and hosts
  • Take only photos and leave no rubbish
