Welcome to Enga Tour Guide

Enga Tour Guide is a locally run tourism business based in Wabag. We help visitors reach the best places in Enga Province safely and comfortably. Our team provides assistant scouts for mountain tracks, transport around the province and lodge accommodation for tourists.
